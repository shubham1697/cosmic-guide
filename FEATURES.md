# Feature Overview

This document describes the core features of the CosmicGuide application.

---

## Daily Horoscope

Users receive personalized daily predictions.

Categories:

* general
* love
* career
* health

---

## Fortune Generator

Users request personalized fortunes.

Inputs:

* zodiac sign
* topic (love/career)
* mood

Output generated by AI.

---

## Tarot Reading

Users draw a tarot card.

System randomly selects a tarot card and generates interpretation.

---

## Compatibility Checker

Users select two zodiac signs.

System returns compatibility score and explanation.

Example:

Scorpio + Taurus
Compatibility: 82%

---

## Personality Insights

AI generates personality traits based on zodiac sign.

Examples:

* strengths
* weaknesses
* emotional patterns

---

## Kundli Matcher

Users enter birth details for two people.

System calculates:

* Gun Milan score
* compatibility insights
* dosha detection

---

## Astrology Stories

Content feed containing astrology articles.

Examples:

* lucky signs of the week
* zodiac personality guides
* relationship advice
