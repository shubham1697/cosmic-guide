# Kundli Matching

Kundli matching evaluates compatibility between two individuals based on Vedic astrology.

---

## Ashta Koota System

Maximum score: 36 points.

Factors evaluated:

Varna
Vashya
Tara
Yoni
Graha Maitri
Gana
Bhakoot
Nadi

---

## Interpretation

Below 18 → Not recommended
18–24 → Average compatibility
24–32 → Good compatibility
32–36 → Excellent compatibility

---

## Additional Analysis

Optional analysis includes:

* Manglik Dosha
* planetary positions
* emotional compatibility

AI can generate explanation based on the score.
